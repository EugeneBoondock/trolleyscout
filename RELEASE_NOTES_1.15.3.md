# Trolley Scout 1.15.3 (39)

## Consumer app

- Marketplace now sits directly below Dashboard in the drawer and beside Dashboard in primary navigation.
- Marketplace has a clear page title, cache-first loading, an active refresh state, and a retry path when a live request fails.
- Product images open in a swipeable gallery with image count, zoom support, sold-out status, and a direct product link.
- Catalogue readers use the highest published page images, support page-by-page browsing, and zoom up to 500% on web.
- Stores now support favourites, a Favourites tab, and an “Enter store” action. Each store page shows its current deals and catalogues.
- Store maps provide turn-by-turn navigation links that work across supported countries.
- Dashboard Stories appear below “Money you kept”. Store catalogues play page by page before current deals, with segmented progress at the top.
- Retail coverage now includes Jet, retailers listed by Kimbino and Catalogue Specials, and country-aware mobile network providers. South African coverage includes MTN, Telkom, Cell C, rain, and Vodacom.
- ASICS now has a scheduled official catalogue check. Products are published as deals only when ASICS states both the current price and a higher previous price.
- Sold-out data is read from supported commerce feeds and shown across Marketplace, Window Shopping, Dashboard, Stories, share cards, and Mr Scout cards.
- Mr Scout is available in the centre of the consumer app navigation and on the consumer website. It uses GPT-5.4 mini and can return verified deal cards, catalogue cards, images, and source links.
- Mr Scout can privately use the signed-in shopper’s basket, saved deals, Window Shopping saves, favourite stores, followed stores, saved properties, and recent property searches.
- Mr Scout remains absent from the Trolley Scout for Business app.

## Trolley Scout for Business

- The business Android app and `org.trolleyscout.co.za` share the same approved-business workspace model and visual system.
- Business access is approval-only. Public account creation is blocked on the business website and in the business app.
- A consumer choosing the Organisation subscription completes a business application with contact, registration, store, and business details.
- Applications appear in the admin console with subscription status and review actions.
- Approval creates the business workspace and sends the owner an access email linking to `org.trolleyscout.co.za`.
- Approved owners sign in with their Trolley Scout account, finish shop setup, manage locations, upload media, and publish deals, promotions, specials, catalogues, and Window Shopping posts.
- Business content supports draft, review, approval, publication, and performance reporting states.
- Access remains tied to admin approval and an active Organisation subscription.
